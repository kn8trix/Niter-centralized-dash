About NITER

The National Institute of Textile Engineering and Research (NITER) is a premier constituent institute of the University of Dhaka, run under the management of the Bangladesh Textile Mills Association (BTMA) under the Ministry of Textiles and Jute. NITER is dedicated to producing world-class engineers, designers, and industrial leaders across engineering and technological disciplines.
